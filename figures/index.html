Default view opens first. Figures 1 and 2 load their saved compositions; other figure numbers start from Default view and can be arranged and copied.
One finger or mouse rotates. Two fingers steer by moving together, roll by twisting, and zoom by pinching. Free positioning holds the exact orientation when released, so you can scroll down and copy it; turning Free off restores magnetic snapping. Labels stay at their zoomed-in text size. Hold a visible label until its outline completes, then drag it to pin it; Reset returns it beside its anchor.
